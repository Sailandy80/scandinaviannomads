Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data Scandinavian Nomads collects and how it is used.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, we store your email address to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email or by contacting us, and we will delete your address.

Contact form

When you send a message, we receive your name, email address and message so we can reply. We do not use it for anything else.

Affiliate links and cookies

This site itself does not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you click an affiliate link, the partner's website may set cookies to register that you came from us. See the partner's own privacy policy for details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Contact connect@scandinaviannomads.com.
