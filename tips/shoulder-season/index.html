Shoulder season: the secret to five-star trips for less

When to go to get great weather, fewer crowds and lower prices.

Published 23 November 2026

Shoulder season is the period just before or after peak season. It is the single most powerful tool for luxury travel on a budget.

What you gain

  • Lower prices on hotels and often flights.
  • Fewer crowds at the places you came to see.
  • Better service, because staff are less stretched.
  • Better availability, including the nicer rooms.

When is shoulder season?

It depends on the destination, but as a rough guide:

  • Southern Europe: April to early June and mid-September to October.
  • Northern Europe and Scandinavia: May to early June and September.
  • Japan: outside cherry blossom and autumn-leaf peaks; early summer and winter can be good value.
  • Southern Hemisphere destinations such as Cape Town: their autumn and spring are our spring and autumn.

What to watch out for

  • Local holidays and festivals can make shoulder dates suddenly expensive. Check the calendar.
  • Opening hours may be reduced in seasonal destinations.
  • Weather is less predictable. Pack layers and keep one flexible day.

The budget-luxury takeaway: move your trip a few weeks and you can often afford a better hotel for the same budget.
