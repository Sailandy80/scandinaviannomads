Pack light, travel well: the carry-on capsule

A simple capsule wardrobe that fits in a cabin bag and looks good from a city break to a five-star dinner.

Published 23 November 2026

Travelling with only a carry-on saves checked-bag fees, time at the airport and the risk of lost luggage. It also feels freer. Here is a capsule that works for most city trips of up to a week.

The capsule

  • Neutral colours that combine. Navy, grey, beige, white and black all work together.
  • 2 trousers or skirts, one smart.
  • 4–5 tops, including one smart shirt or blouse.
  • 1 warm layer, such as a merino or cashmere sweater.
  • 1 jacket that works for both day and dinner. Wear it on the plane.
  • 2 pairs of shoes: comfortable walkers and something smarter. Wear the bulkiest pair while travelling.
  • Merino underwear and socks, which can be washed and dried overnight.

Packing tricks

  • Packing cubes keep things organised and compress clothes.
  • Decant toiletries into small, refillable bottles that follow the liquid rules for your departure airport.
  • One adapter and a multi-port charger instead of several chargers.
  • Leave space for anything you might buy.

Check your airline's rules

Cabin-bag size and weight limits vary a lot between airlines, and budget carriers often charge for anything larger than an under-seat bag. Check before you buy a ticket and when you buy a new bag.

The budget-luxury takeaway: a small, well-chosen wardrobe makes you look good, move faster and pay less.
