Fine dining at lunch prices

Why lunch is the smartest way to eat at great restaurants, and how to find the best deals.

Published 23 November 2026

As a former restaurant manager, here is a trade secret: many of the best kitchens in the world quietly offer their cooking at a much lower price at lunchtime. Same chefs, same produce, often a shorter menu.

Why restaurants do it

Lunch fills tables that would otherwise be quiet, introduces new guests to the restaurant, and uses the same mise en place the kitchen prepares for dinner. For you, it means a taste of a great restaurant for a fraction of the evening price.

How to find lunch deals

  • Check the restaurant's own website for "lunch menu", "set lunch" or "menu du jour".
  • Look at weekdays. The best-value lunch menus are usually Monday to Friday.
  • Look for the dish of the day. In Portugal it is the prato do dia, in Mexico the comida corrida, in France the formule.
  • Book ahead for popular places, even at lunch.

Make it feel like an occasion

  • Choose a window table or a terrace.
  • Order a glass of wine by the glass rather than a bottle.
  • Take your time. A long lunch is one of travel's great luxuries.

Then keep dinner simple

Make lunch your main meal and have something light in the evening: a market picnic, street food or tapas. You eat better and spend less.

The budget-luxury takeaway: if a restaurant is on your wish list, check its lunch menu first.
