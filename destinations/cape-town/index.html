South Africa · Africa & Middle East

Cape Town

Mountains, ocean, wine country and world-class dining at pleasantly local prices.

Why go

Cape Town is where budget luxury shines brightest. With a favourable exchange rate for most European visitors, you can stay in a stylish guesthouse, eat at some of the continent's best restaurants and spend days in the Cape Winelands, all for what a basic trip costs in many European capitals.

Best time to visit

  • October to April: summer, long days and beach weather. Peak season is December to January.
  • February to March: warm, slightly calmer, and harvest time in the winelands.
  • May to September: cooler and wetter, but green, quiet and great value. Whale season on the coast starts in winter.

Where to stay

Choose a boutique guesthouse in areas like Gardens, Tamboerskloof, De Waterkant or Camps Bay. Many offer pools, mountain views and personal service at mid-range prices. In the winelands, a night or two at a wine estate guesthouse is a classic splurge that often costs less than you think.

What to eat

Cape Malay curries, braai (barbecue), fresh line fish, and tasting menus in Cape Town and Franschhoek. Wine tastings are inexpensive compared with Europe.

How to save

  • Book tasting menus at lunch where available, and eat at the city's food markets.
  • Do the free headline experiences. Lion's Head and Table Mountain hikes, Sea Point promenade, Muizenberg beach.
  • Rent a car for the Cape Peninsula and winelands and split the cost.
  • Travel in shoulder season. Prices and crowds drop noticeably outside December and January.

Safety

Use common sense as in any big city: use registered ride-hailing at night, do not display valuables, and follow local advice on areas and hiking routes.

Sample 3-day itinerary

Day 1 – City and mountain. Table Mountain early (cableway or hike), Bo-Kaap, sunset at Lion's Head or Signal Hill.

Day 2 – Cape Peninsula. Chapman's Peak Drive, Cape Point, the penguins at Boulders Beach, seafood in Kalk Bay.

Day 3 – Winelands. Stellenbosch or Franschhoek tastings and a long estate lunch.

Budget luxury score: 8.8

Few places give you this much luxury per krone. Guesthouses, food and wine are outstanding value.
