## Why go

Hong Kong mixes world-class luxury with some of the best cheap thrills in Asia. You can drink in a skyline view from the Star Ferry for a few Hong Kong dollars, eat dim sum at famously affordable Michelin-recognised restaurants, and hike green mountain trails within an hour of the city centre.

## Best time to visit

- **October to early December:** the classic window, with lower humidity and clearer views.
- **March to April:** pleasant, though often hazier.
- **June to September:** hot, humid and typhoon season. Hotels are cheaper, but plan with flexibility.

## Where to stay

Hotel rooms are small and pricey, so value is about **location and the view**, not square metres. Tsim Sha Tsui and Sheung Wan offer good mid-range options close to transport. A great budget-luxury trick: stay somewhere modest and book **one night at a harbour-view luxury hotel** at the end of the trip, or simply visit a five-star hotel lobby lounge for afternoon tea.

## What to eat

Dim sum, roast goose and char siu, wonton noodles, egg tarts, pineapple buns and milk tea at a *cha chaan teng*. Dai pai dong street-style eateries and cooked-food centres are cheap and atmospheric.

## How to save

- **Get an Octopus card.** It covers the MTR, buses, trams, ferries and many shops.
- **Ride the Star Ferry and the "Ding Ding" tram.** Classic experiences for very little money.
- **Hike for free.** Dragon's Back and the Lion Rock trail give spectacular views without a ticket.
- **Have a set lunch at a fine restaurant.** Many upscale kitchens offer lunch menus at a fraction of dinner prices.

## Sample 3-day itinerary

**Day 1 – Central and the Peak.** Sheung Wan antique streets, Central, the Peak (walk up or take the tram early), Star Ferry at dusk.

**Day 2 – Kowloon.** Dim sum breakfast, markets in Mong Kok, Tsim Sha Tsui promenade and the evening light show across the harbour.

**Day 3 – Nature and islands.** Dragon's Back hike to Shek O beach, or a ferry to Lamma Island for seafood by the water.

## Budget luxury score: 7.8

Accommodation is the expensive part, but food, transport and views are some of the best value anywhere.
