## Why go

Mexico City combines museums, architecture, parks and one of the world's great food scenes. You can eat outstanding tacos for a couple of euros and have a celebrated tasting menu the same day, and stay in a design hotel in a tree-lined neighbourhood for mid-range prices.

## Best time to visit

- **Late October to April:** the dry season, with sunny days and cool evenings. Día de Muertos (around 1–2 November) is spectacular but busy.
- **March to May:** the warmest months.
- **June to September:** rainy season, usually short afternoon showers; fewer visitors.

## Where to stay

Base yourself in **Roma Norte or Condesa** for walkable streets, cafés and boutique hotels, or **Polanco** for a more upscale feel near Chapultepec. Small design hotels and well-run guesthouses here offer excellent value.

## What to eat

Tacos al pastor, tlacoyos, tamales, mole, chilaquiles for breakfast, mezcal, and seasonal specialities. The city's markets and street stalls are an essential part of the experience.

## How to save

- **Eat the *comida corrida*.** The set lunch at local fondas is a classic bargain.
- **Book the famous restaurants for lunch** or their tasting menus well ahead, and go à la carte elsewhere.
- **Use ride-hailing apps** for convenient, fairly priced trips; the metro is very cheap but busy.
- **Visit museums on free days.** Several museums have free entry for everyone on certain days; check before you go.

## Practical tips

The city is at about 2,240 metres altitude, so take it easy on day one and drink plenty of water. Use registered taxis or apps at night.

## Sample 3-day itinerary

**Day 1 – Centro Histórico.** Zócalo, the cathedral, Templo Mayor, Palacio de Bellas Artes, dinner in Roma.

**Day 2 – Chapultepec and Polanco.** Museo Nacional de Antropología, Chapultepec Park, a long lunch in Polanco.

**Day 3 – Coyoacán and Xochimilco.** Frida Kahlo's Casa Azul (book ahead), Coyoacán market, an afternoon on the trajineras of Xochimilco.

## Budget luxury score: 8.5

Design hotels, world-class food and culture at mid-range prices. A budget-luxury favourite.
