Luxury is a way of travelling, not a price

Scandinavian Nomads started with a simple observation: the best travel moments are rarely the most expensive ones. A sea-view cabin in Lofoten, a long lunch in Lisbon, the Star Ferry at dusk in Hong Kong. With the right timing and a few insider tricks, you can travel in real comfort without paying full luxury prices.

The site is written by a Norwegian who grew up between mountains and fjords and has since visited more than 90 countries. Over 25 years he has worked as cabin crew, purser and base manager in aviation, as a destination manager opening and running destinations in several countries, as a restaurant manager, and in hospitality at sea. That means the tips here come from both sides of the counter: as a traveller, and as someone who has run the hotels, flights and restaurants you book.

The budget-luxury philosophy

  • Spend where it matters. A great bed, a great view and one memorable meal beat ten average ones.
  • Timing is everything. Shoulder season and lunch menus are the two biggest secrets in travel.
  • Be honest. If something is overpriced, we say so. Sponsored content is always clearly labelled.
