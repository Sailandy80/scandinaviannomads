Affiliate disclosure

Scandinavian Nomads is free to read. To keep it that way, we may earn money in the following ways:

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a booking or purchase, we may earn a small commission from the provider. It costs you nothing extra, and sometimes you get the same or a better price than going direct.

Affiliate links are marked with a note next to them. We only recommend hotels, services and products we would use ourselves, and a commission never decides what we recommend or how we rate a destination.

Sponsored content

We may occasionally publish content created in cooperation with a hotel, tourist board or brand. Such content is always clearly labelled "Sponsored" (in Norwegian: annonse) at the top, in line with the Norwegian Marketing Control Act (markedsføringsloven) and the guidance of the Norwegian Consumer Authority (Forbrukertilsynet).

Independence

Our opinions are our own. If something is overpriced or disappointing, we will say so, sponsored or not.

Questions? Contact us at connect@scandinaviannomads.com.
